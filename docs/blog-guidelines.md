# Blog Content Guidelines — BairesDev Solutions

## Purpose
Every blog post published on bairesdevsolution.com must demonstrate genuine expertise in our service areas. Posts are written for **business owners, marketers, and entrepreneurs** — not developers. Content must be actionable, specific, and directly relevant to the services we offer.

## Content Rules

### Tone
- Direct, confident, and honest — no hype or fluff
- Write like a trusted expert talking to a client, not a textbook
- Use "you" to address the reader directly
- Contractions are fine (don't, can't, you'll)

### Structure (mandatory)
1. **Hook** — open with a relatable problem or surprising fact (1–2 sentences)
2. **The core problem** — explain why this matters and what goes wrong without solving it (1 short paragraph)
3. **The solution** — step-by-step or numbered approach (main body, 3–5 sections with ## headings)
4. **Common mistakes** — at least 2–3 things people get wrong (### sub-section or bullet list)
5. **Takeaway + CTA** — close with the most important insight and a soft call to action to contact BairesDev Solutions

### Length
- Minimum: 900 words
- Target: 1,000–1,200 words
- Maximum: 1,400 words

### Headings
- Use `##` for main sections (3–5 per post)
- Use `###` for sub-sections where needed
- Headings must be descriptive and include a keyword naturally

### Lists
- Use bullet lists (`-`) for 3+ parallel items
- Use numbered lists for sequential steps
- Never use a list with only 1 or 2 items — write prose instead

### Specificity rules
- Include real tool names, specific settings, or feature names where relevant
- Include at least one concrete example or scenario
- Include at least one stat or number (can be estimated/general, e.g. "most stores see a 20–40% lift")
- Never make vague claims like "this will improve your business" without saying how

### What to avoid
- Do not start with "In today's digital landscape..."
- Do not use passive voice excessively
- Do not pad with generic filler paragraphs
- Do not repeat the title in the first sentence
- No em dashes used mid-sentence for decoration

## SEO Rules (per post)

### Meta title
- 50–60 characters
- Must include the primary keyword near the front
- Format: `[Keyword-rich title] | BairesDev Solutions` OR just the title if it's already keyword-rich
- Do NOT include pipes or brand name if it would push over 60 chars

### Meta description
- 140–155 characters
- Must summarise what the reader will learn
- Must include the primary keyword
- Should have a natural call to read (e.g. "Here's how to..." or "Find out why...")

### Primary keyword
- Appears in: title, first 100 words, at least one ## heading, meta title, meta description
- Do not keyword-stuff — use it naturally 3–5 times total

### Secondary keywords
- 4 additional related keywords/phrases used naturally throughout the post

### Internal CTA
Every post ends with a soft pitch:
> "If you're looking to [solve the problem covered in this post], BairesDev Solutions can help. [Contact us] to discuss your project."

## Image Guidelines
- Every post gets one AI-generated cover image
- Image must be professional, clean, and directly relevant to the topic
- Aspect ratio: 16:9
- Style: photorealistic digital render or clean flat illustration — no cartoons
- No text in the image
- No people/faces — use abstract, interface, or concept visuals
